# Code of conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. The full
text lives at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ — that
page is the canonical version; the summary below is just a reading aid.

In short:

- Be welcoming and respectful. People of every background, identity and
  experience level are here to work on a small calendar extension.
- Assume good faith, accept constructive criticism, and give it without
  making it personal. Focus on what's best for the people using the
  software.
- Not welcome: harassment or insults, sexualised language or attention,
  trolling and deliberate derailment, doxxing or publishing someone's
  private information, and any other conduct that would be out of place
  in a professional setting.
- Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and PRs that
  break these rules, and may warn or ban repeat offenders, as laid out in
  the enforcement ladder of the full text.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes the project unwelcome, open a private
report through the repository's GitHub Security Advisories page
(https://github.com/mlkonrad/litsycal/security/advisories/new) — despite
the name it reaches the maintainer privately — and describe what happened
and where. Reports are read in confidence.
